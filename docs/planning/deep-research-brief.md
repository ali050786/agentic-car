### Context — the product being monetized
"agentic-car" is a working, deployed (but essentially zero-user, zero-revenue) web app that generates social-media carousel posts (primarily for LinkedIn, secondarily Instagram) using a multi-agent AI pipeline. Its differentiators versus typical tools: (1) a conversational "orchestrator" agent that routes copy, layout/palette, and image edits from a single chat instruction; (2) a research agent that pulls real-world data and citations (Tavily) into the carousel content; (3) four distinct visual templates including an AI hand-drawn "doodle/sketch" style rendered via an image model (Replicate Flux); (4) a persistent memory of the user's brand/design preferences; (5) one-click SVG export straight into Figma. It is currently a free platform with no payment system. Built solo.

### The decision
Recommend a monetization path: (a) the most winnable, willing-to-pay buyer segment within or adjacent to LinkedIn personal-brand creators; (b) a pricing model and price point grounded in what comparable tools charge and what this segment actually pays; (c) the geography to launch pricing in first; (d) the sharpest differentiation wedge given the competitive field. Also flag the biggest risks to a solo, bootstrapped operator.
